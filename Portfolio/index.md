# Sovereign Realms: portfolio stills

3840 x 2160, captured in-engine (vl.Shot / vl.Record) from the packaged Development build (UE 5.8) running at 3840 x 2160. Each image: a lossless PNG master and a JPG (quality 92, sRGB, 4:4:4) for the web. Clean set: no HUD, no fog of war, no gold see-through outline (vl.Opt.Silhouette 0), no white hit flash (vl.Opt.HitFlash 0); the level's midday sun except the two golden-hour shots (vl.Sun -11 35 7 4300, no clouds). Capture scripts: Tools/portfolio_stills.py, Tools/portfolio_shots.py. Contact sheets: contact_sheet.jpg, gameplay_ui/contact_sheet.jpg.
